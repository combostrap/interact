title: Anchor (Link)
---


This component generates HTML links.

It's used:

* as [Markdown component](../reference/markdown-component.md) for links.
* in your [module page (Tsx)](../reference/page-module.md), to support
  the [base configuration](../reference/site.md#base)

## Best Practice

### Use the same structure as your app

Example:

* https://example.com/app/api-key
* https://example.com/docs/api-key
